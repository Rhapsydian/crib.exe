## Goal

Resolve session 46's finding that `selfState` `heatAbove`/`heatBelow`
read the wrong quantity, and decide how to handle `cold-call`'s
non-terminating fight.

## Design decisions resolved live

1. **Triggers read in-combat Trace, not run Heat.** Decisive argument:
   run Heat *cannot change during a fight*, so a run-Heat condition would
   be a per-fight constant — the same always/never degeneracy, relabelled.
   Only the in-combat value moves mid-fight. Also made this a content
   pass with no `CombatOptions` plumbing.
2. **Rename the accumulator to Trace**, converting to Heat at fight end.
   The name was the root cause: calling it `heat` while it emphatically
   was not the run's Heat produced the bug, the enemy leftover, and my own
   mischaracterisation during diagnosis.
3. **Trace generation spread to every reader archetype**, with Exploit
   leaning hardest into the tradeoff (the user's call — it is the
   archetype's identity).
4. **Points cooldown, not a Haste clamp**, for the turn engine — so Merge
   keeps improving those pieces. Plus a **loud, fatal loop detector**
   rather than a silent cap.
5. **Enemy Trace pressure** via a per-piece `tracePressure` field, on
   thematic hunters only. Not a contradiction of session 43's player-only
   rule: enemies still have no Trace *resource*, they just make you
   visible.

## The through-line: tempo, not damage

Four separate problems this session turned out to be turn economy:

1. **Cold Call's self-Haste** — Merge lifted it to the initiative
   threshold, one fire bought a whole turn, grants compounded into a 4GB
   heap.
2. **`MIN_INITIATIVE_THRESHOLD` at 1** — every point granted a turn.
   Notably a bigger *enemy* buff than a player one (DNS Poisoning sits in
   three enemy loadouts, only three classes can draw it), so fixing it
   raised player win rates across the board.
3. **The Quarantine Ward** — `total-quarantine` nudges initiative on
   every DoT *or HoT* tick, the nudge flat and magnitude-independent.
   Four changes moved its ablation delta only 8.4pp → 5.7pp; a test at
   scaler 0.4 (a 65% cut) moved the layer 0.8pp. Magnitude was never the
   lever.
4. **DNS Poisoning** spends `UNCOMMON.burst` (a damage constant) as a
   threshold reduction. Still open.

Every tuning tool the project has targets damage. Written up in
`BACKLOG.md` with a proposed per-enemy initiative-threshold override.

## Mistakes made and caught

Recorded because the pattern matters more than any single fix.

- **`always` + `traceCost` three times.** My own plan said Trace belongs
  on conditional triggers; I then put it on `static-noise` and
  `contagion-protocol`. At ~12.7 fires/fight even cost 1 is ~13 Trace.
  It cost Blackhat ~16pp (60% of its runs heat-maxed) and made the
  Quarantine Ward layer 2's spike. Now a structural test, not a comment.
- **A vacuous regression test.** The preserved `hang-state.json` fixture
  "passed" after the rename because its serialized `heatBelow` matched no
  case any more, so `cold-call` never armed. Migrating the fixture showed
  the detector firing at hand 2 with 213 consecutive turns.
- **Blackhat never needed a buff.** The proposed Rest multiplier would
  have compensated for a wound I had inflicted hours earlier, and would
  have killed its Merge economy (0.03 merges/run) doing so.
- **Two failed tunes, reverted rather than kept**: the Quarantine Ward's
  scaler (provably inert) and `RETURN_TO_SENDER_RATIO` (0.25→0.3→0.4
  produced 8.0→9.3→8.7%, all inside ±3pp).

## The finding that ends the session

Ghost measured worst at 8.0%. Raising its passive's conversion rate did
nothing, while a `wardCounter` change measured +8pp — a contradiction I
could not explain, and said so rather than papering over.

Chasing it down: **`--acquisition=floor` gave Ghost 24.7%, `synergy` gave
8.0%.** The "smart" heuristic made it three times worse. Cause:
`acquireSubroutineWithSwap` ranked installed pieces on the ladder alone,
and `ward` is not in `CREDIT_CAPABLE_PAYLOAD_KINDS` — so Ghost's
Steganography was evicted in **72% of runs (42/150 kept vs 150/150 under
floor)**, and Return to Sender's only reachable hook is ward absorption.
The ladder was throwing away the class's engine.

Both earlier results then explain each other: `wardCounter` worked
because it is *in* the credit-capable set and therefore stopped the
eviction — not because of its ratio. Raising a conversion rate is
worthless when the thing being converted has been discarded.

It also explains why swap-out was the one heuristic with no positive
evidence in session 46's ablation (42/240 with, 47/240 without): not
neutral, but helping several classes while destroying one.

**Consequence: most of this session's balance work was calibrated against
a broken instrument.** After the fix, synergy beats floor for every class
for the first time, and the roster reads 10.7–32.7 with four classes
*above* the 10–20 target — including Saboteur at 30.0% *with* its nerf
already applied. Deliberately not re-tuned; a fresh pass belongs in its
own session against a trustworthy heuristic.
