# Session 32 — 2026-08-27

`/decision-session`, direct follow-up to sessions 30-31 in the same
sitting — built a small validation sample of real Mod content, at the
user's own suggestion, specifically to catch gaps in the shape/catalog
before scoping implementation. No code — docs only.

## Goal

Confirm sessions 30-31's design (engine mechanism split, hook-point
catalog) actually holds up against real content ideas, before committing
to implementation checkpoints — motivated directly by this project's own
history: session 17's original Phase 2 scope was written before session
12's concrete subroutine content existed, and had to be rescoped once it
turned out most of the real 18-subroutine catalog didn't fit the
original plan.

## Key decisions

1. **Scope the pass small and deliberate, not launch-sized** — target
   ~15-20 Mods, aimed specifically at touching every hook point and both
   engine buckets at least once, rather than letting coverage happen
   organically or trying to reach a real launch-sized pool. Same
   relationship session 12's 18 starting subroutines had to session 22's
   later 60-piece expansion.
2. **Drafted 17 Mods across 3 rarity tiers**, deliberately including two
   stress-test items aimed at finding gaps rather than just filling out
   the list:
   - **Found a real gap**: a trigger-mechanism-affinity Mod
     ("Overclocked Accumulator" — ease an Accumulator's threshold)
     didn't fit any of the 11 catalogued hooks. `onFire` only fires
     after a trigger is already satisfied; nothing touched the
     readiness check itself, even though "specific trigger mechanism"
     was named as one of three core Mod categories back at session 30's
     very first round.
   - **Confirmed a near-miss wasn't a gap**: "permanently raise max Heat
     capacity" ("Backup Generator") looked like it might need a new
     `onRunStart` hook, but `onModAcquired` already covers it — a
     class-exclusive Mod granted at run start and a found Mod picked up
     mid-run both route through the same acquisition moment.
3. **Added a 12th hook, `onTriggerEvaluate`** — chainable, fires during
   `triggers.ts`'s per-subroutine readiness check, letting a Mod adjust
   the effective threshold/progress requirement before readiness is
   decided. Confirmed by the user before continuing the draft.
4. **Final 17-Mod list confirmed as a good starting spread** in one
   round — 7 commons, 6 uncommons, 4 rares, covering all 12 hooks and
   both engine buckets (including a real reactive-subroutine-shaped Mod
   and the granted-subroutine mechanism from session 31).

## Notable problem-solving notes

- The whole point of this session was validated directly: deliberately
  drafting toward stress-test cases (not just "plausible Mod ideas")
  found a real gap in one try. A content pass aimed at *filling out* a
  catalog and one aimed at *finding holes in* a catalog are different
  exercises, even though they look similar from the outside — worth
  remembering the framing (session 17's own lesson) when scoping any
  future "does this design hold up" pass, not just this one.
- The `onRunStart`-vs-`onModAcquired` near-miss is a good example of a
  question worth actually tracing through the pipeline rather than
  pattern-matching on "sounds like it needs a new hook" — the two
  acquisition paths (class-exclusive at run start, found mid-run) turned
  out to already share one mechanism.
