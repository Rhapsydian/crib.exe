This is the release candidate: everything planned for launch is in, and from here it's fixes from your reports. What's new since the star ratings update (the previous notes):

Heads up for testers: the testing switches (?unlockall and ?resetprofile) are gone from the live game. To start over, use Settings → Erase all progress (export your progress first if you want it back).

Highlights

  • Sound. Every card is a keystroke, and the rest of the game has its own short sound effects. Volume and mute are in Settings.
  • Settings, Records and About. Three new screens from the main menu.
  • Your progress as a code. Export it, carry it to another device or browser, import it back.
  • A Discord for bug reports, ideas and Daily scores.
  • An icon, and an app. crib.exe has its own icon, and you can add it to your phone's home screen to play full screen.
  • Version numbers, starting at v0.9.0 (bottom of the main menu).

Sound

  • Cards are keystrokes. Each card plays one of ten recorded key presses at random, never the same twice in a row, so a hand sounds like typing. Now and then a card is a quick double tap. Your cards sound a little higher than theirs.
  • The rest is the game's own synth: a hand starting, the cut, the table clearing, peg points and show counts, turns, subroutines firing, Mods and Burners, alarms raising Heat, Breached and Contained, map moves, Caches, rewards, and a chime for a Flawless rating.
  • Volume and mute in Settings. No music.

Settings

  • Sound: volume and mute.
  • Turn speed: slow, normal or fast (the same as the » button in a game).
  • Show tips again (moved here from the main menu).
  • Progress: export it as a code to copy, import a code (it shows what the code holds before replacing anything), or erase everything and start over. Your sound and speed settings are kept.

Records

Your history in one place: runs and victories, runs and wins by class with your best Security Level and deepest layer, fights won (elites, gatekeepers, Flawless fights), a career Cribbage rating across every hand you've played, Play Cribbage results by difficulty, and your Daily streak, best streak and best score. Class, fight and Cribbage records count from this version on.

About, patch notes and the Discord

  • About: credits, how the game was made (agentic development, using Claude Code), and the source on GitHub.
  • What's new: after an update, the main menu tells you what changed, once. Every version's notes are on the Patch notes page in About.
  • Discord: the button is on the main menu. Report bugs in #bug-reports, share ideas in #feedback, post Daily scores in #daily-runs.

Saved games and updates

If an update changes the rules a saved run, Daily Run or Cribbage game was played under, it's cleared and the game tells you, instead of failing when you try to resume. If it was that day's scored Daily attempt, you get the attempt back.

Learn to play

  • The discard lesson shows your keep and the coach's side by side, in numbers, like the coach in a game.
  • The lesson text is tidied up.

Fixes

  • The review's costliest mistakes no longer list a choice the coach counts as its own.
  • Cards in the log are spaced evenly.
  • Tips no longer pop up while a saved game catches up.
  • Play Cribbage resumes where you left off.
  • On iPhone, a double tap no longer zooms the page.
