# Devlog: crib.exe release candidate is up

Title: **crib.exe release candidate: help test before 1.0**

Type: Release (itch's devlog "type" field). Attach `gameplay.gif` as the post's image.

---

**crib.exe is a hacking roguelite where every fight is a game of Cribbage, and it's ready for testing before 1.0.**

You break into a network four layers deep. Each layer is a branching map of fights, Shops, Events and Caches, with a gatekeeper at the top, and every move raises your Heat. Every fight is a game of Cribbage against the system you're hacking: your pegging, hand and crib points fill your initiative, and when it fills, your loadout of subroutines fires top to bottom to fill your Breach before the enemy fills its Containment.

### What's in it

- 6 classes, each with its own Mod and starting loadout
- 52 subroutines across four archetypes, one per suit
- Dozens of Mods, 24 Burners and 24 Events
- 49 enemies, including gatekeepers that each bend a rule of their own
- A Daily Run with the same class and twists for everyone, one scored attempt a day
- A guided tutorial, a coach that rates your Cribbage play, and plain Cribbage with short lessons if you're new to the game

### What "release candidate" means

Everything planned for launch is in. From here until 1.0 it's fixes and balance from your reports, and your progress carries over to 1.0.

Recent changes from tester reports: your loadout is now visible on the reward and Shop screens, Event choices with odds roll on screen, the Cribbage rating was made fairer, and a subroutine combination that could chain a dozen turns in a row was fixed.

### How you can help

Play a run or a Daily, and tell us what you find: bugs, anything confusing, anything that feels too strong or too weak. The quickest way is the Discord: https://discord.gg/HxCry3uRpa (#bug-reports, #feedback). Comments here work too. If you report a bug, the build number on the About page helps a lot.

It plays in the browser on desktop and phones. Thanks for playing!
