Release candidate. crib.exe is in final testing before 1.0: everything planned for launch is in, and what's left is fixes from your reports. Your progress carries over to 1.0. Found a bug or have an idea? Tell us on the Discord (#bug-reports, #feedback) or in the comments below.

Break into a network, four layers deep. Every fight is a game of Cribbage.

You're a hacker working through a guarded network. Each layer is a branching map of fights, Shops, Quiet Hosts, Caches and Events, with a gatekeeper at the top, and every move raises your Heat. Max it out and you're traced.

How a fight works. Play Cribbage against the system you're breaking into. The points you score in pegging, your hand and your crib fill your initiative, and when it fills you take a turn: every subroutine in your loadout fires, top to bottom, building your Breach. Fill it before the enemy's subroutines fill its Containment.

Build your kit. Pick up new subroutines after each win and order them so they feed each other. Add Mods that bend the rules for the rest of the run, and one-use Burners for when a fight turns. Refactor spare copies to rank your subroutines up.

What's in it

  • 6 classes, each with its own Mod and starting loadout
  • 52 subroutines across four archetypes, one per suit: Exploit, Daemon, Overclock and Honeypot
  • Dozens of Mods, 24 Burners and 24 Events
  • 49 enemies, from regular fights to elites and gatekeepers that bend a rule of their own
  • A Daily Run: the same class and twists for everyone, one scored attempt a day
  • A guided tutorial, and a coach that rates your Cribbage play
  • Plain Cribbage too: four difficulty levels, a coach, and short lessons if you're new to the game

New to Cribbage?

Choose Learn to play on the main menu: five short lessons, then a game with a coach. The tutorial run explains the roguelite side as you play.

Controls

Tap or click. Plays on desktop and on phones (portrait). Sound and turn speed are in Settings.

Saves

Your progress is saved in your browser. It's separate from the copy at cribexe.com: to move it across, use Settings → Export progress, then Import on the other.
