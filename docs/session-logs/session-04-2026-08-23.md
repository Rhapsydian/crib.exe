# Session 4 — 2026-08-23

`/decision-session`, a short, focused one: mapping which of session 3's
6 subroutine trigger families each archetype leans into, the one item
session 3 explicitly left unmapped.

Opened with a structural observation rather than a blind brainstorm:
Chained and Always/Cantrip both read as universal, cross-cutting tools
(any archetype's subroutines can chain into each other; any archetype
can want a cheap always-on version) rather than archetype-specific —
which conveniently leaves exactly 4 remaining families for 4 archetypes.
Proposed and confirmed in one round:

- **Exploit ↔ Occurrence** — a great cribbage play *is* the
  "vulnerability found," opportunistic and spiky.
- **Malware ↔ Accumulators** — corruption incubating on a steady
  schedule, the clearest pairing of the four.
- **Encryption ↔ Self-state** — defense reacting to your own peril.
- **Root ↔ Enemy-state** — Root's payloads are already enemy-directed,
  so its triggers watch the enemy too.

Flagged the Encryption/Root split specifically as the one real judgment
call in the mapping (could've gone the other way) rather than presenting
the whole thing as equally obvious — confirmed as proposed. This is a
primary-affinity mapping, not an exclusivity rule.

Updated `DESIGN.md`'s Subroutine Trigger Catalog with the mapping inline
and trimmed the resolved item from Open Questions; `BACKLOG.md`'s Phase
0 item marked done. Still docs-only, no code.
