## Problem-solving notes

- **Init Script, 600 runs a variant** (natural pickup, then every run
  forced to own it from the start):

  | Variant | Natural victory | Forced victory | Data paid (forced) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 5, next Mod only (before) | 29.7% | 31.2% | 4.6 |
  | Every Mod, 5 | 29.8% | 33.2% | 40 |
  | Every Mod, 7 | 29.7% | 33.0% | 56 |
  | Every Mod, 10 | 29.7% | 32.3% | 80 |

  The forced differences are within noise. A natural pickup sees ~4.8
  later Mods (~49 Data at 10); 24% of runs own it, 75 of 600 bought at
  the Shop. The simulated player ranks Mods by rarity and synergy, not
  by payout, so its pick rate doesn't move with the amount.
