## Decisions (user, live — `docs/authoring-design.md`)

  plain, uncommons conditional, rares build-arounds.
- **S2/S3:** the user had no favourite pieces (the game has never been
  playable), so Claude drafts and the user reacts. The old pool's names
  are reused with new mechanics.
- **S4:** author first, UI after. The interleaved option was offered
  and declined.
- **S5:** an initial set of 36 pieces, 9 per archetype (4/3/2), to grow
  later.
- **S6:** power bands in Credit per turn: common 2.0, uncommon 2.25,
  rare 2.5 in its build (at least 2.0 alone).
- **S7:** one archetype per checkpoint, Exploit → Daemon → Overclock →
  Honeypot.
- **S8:** rares stay inside the model; rule-bending belongs to Mods.
- **S9:** growth is priced in a mid-length fight (host Credit 4, ~8
  hands). This came out of clarifying turns vs hands. The user's
  framing: be mindful of balance, but this pass needn't be perfect.
- **S10:** conversion keeps its Ward bypass, with small steals priced
  against Ward.
- **S11:** Honeypot carries its own skill-scaled offense, and Honeypot
  classes still carry partner credit as a backstop.
- **C1–C6:** bespoke bridge per class (Mods); a +1.0 Credit/turn bridge

  Eidolon (the user's pick over Cryptominer and others), Breacher,

## Problem-solving notes

- **Growth's price depends on fight length** (Memory Leak priced 4.3,
  3.4 or 3.8 by host). That became S9 and Pricing rule 3b.
- **Sequence pieces inherit a neighbour's bonus**: inserting one beside
  Burst Clock credited it with Burst Clock's adjacency. Fixed with a
  neutral spacer, and `priceAddition` gained a numeric placement. Rule
  3c.
- **A Charge partner needs cap to spare** (Zero-Day Chain over Buffer
  Overflow wasted every charge).
- **Absorbed-fuelled conversion inverts skill**: Honeypot kits measured
  retention as low as −1.0. Lower rates didn't help; the fuel source was
  the cause. Fixed with plain Credit alphas (S11) and Tarpit spending
  its own banked Ward. Rule 3d.
- **A Honeypot mirror stalled** (2% threshold wins) until conversion
  could bypass Ward, confirming the user's first watch item.
- **Flat haste is nearly worthless** (0.96); big occasional hastes work.
- The wiring needed content modules to stop importing `pricing.ts`, to
  avoid a classes → subroutines → content → pricing → combat → resolve
  → classes import cycle.
