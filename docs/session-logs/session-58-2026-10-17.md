## Decisions (user, live)

- **M1:** the map shows only the current layer (a passed gatekeeper is
  one-way).
- **M2:** the bench is the inactive storage, **capped at 3**; a full
  bench means choosing a piece to drop. Pieces move between loadout and
  bench any time outside a fight.
- **Map generation:** from mockups (layered / force / schematic, then
  scatter / subnets / hybrid / hex), the user chose **scatter on a faint
  hex backdrop**, nodes centred on hexes and never on neighbouring hexes;
  a longer climb with nothing behind the gatekeeper; **layer sizes
  10/12/12/14**; relays are never dead ends.
- **M7/M8:** a dealt node-type mix with placement rules, and a **Cache**
  node -- a remote dead end with a free reward (a better subroutine
  choice, Data plus Burners, or rarely a Mod on later layers).
- **Heat:** the two-lever pass (allowances ×1.9 to 76/68/61/53, then the
  gatekeepers to the curve). Wraith left high for later tuning.
- **Trace removed** (the user's idea): fights post Heat directly
  (baseline + Heat per Containment %; a loss pays the baseline + 150% of
  the full Containment term); Fuzzer and Jackpot's riders now **feed the
  opponent's gauge**; tracer enemies and Spotlight are **alarms** that add
  Heat directly.
- **Rewards and loadout:** a person's picks are never swapped in for
  them; the loadout drawer edits; a full bench can drop an installed
  piece.
- **Set aside:** Static Shield (overtuned) and Recon Ping (no fog of
  war); Salvage Protocol and Fast Learner gated to classes that can use
  them; every offer skippable; a **Burner arriving at the carry cap can
  be swapped** for one carried.
- **The Shop is bought freely:** all three slates on one screen, buy
  anything affordable (each once), reroll each slate once.
- **Saves:** a replay of the player's answers (Claude's call, stated:
  exact, mid-fight included; doesn't survive a rules/content update).
- **Re-tune without Static Shield**, then push.
