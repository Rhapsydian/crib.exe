## Problem-solving notes

- **Random draws don't promise variety:** a 13-Directive pool still
  repeated within a week; a shuffled deck per cycle (with a boundary
  swap) does.
- **Gross, not net:** Data earned and spent settle between nodes with the
  Shop's costs noted, so spending isn't netted against earning (a test
  checks earned − spent = Data left).
- **Find every 31:** an audit found the limit in pegging, the AI's risk
  counts, the coach's threats and wording, the table, the hand and the
  log; thirty-one Mods follow automatically through the scoring event.
- **Mirror tests separate bugs from effects:** Turncoat Crib cost 3-5
  points a fight (evening out a skilled player's crib edge), not a bug.
- **Check the right exit code:** a commit went in with a failing test
  because the command checked a grep; later commits gate on the tests.
- **Overwrite vs adjust:** a Glitch that set "pairs score 1" erased Pair
  Fold's upside; adjusting on top keeps both.

## After the fourth close-out: one AI and the rating (the user's design, live)

Players reported the fight rating felt low. Talked through live:

- **The old rating was arcane** (the user): a per-choice e^-lost curve,
  discards weighted double; half a point given up scored 58%. Replaced by
  **option B** (the user's pick): points given up per hand, against the AI
  at full strength; **×10, no allowance** (the 1-point allowance was
  chosen, then dropped once B charged small slips only what they cost).
  Only mistakes of a whole point or more are listed (the user).
- **The coach and the AI had drifted** (the user: they should be one set of
  algorithms): the coach judged a play by its expected reply over the
  unseen cards, the AI by a flat "5 or 21 is risky" list and an
  adjacent-rank bonus. Head to head (4,000 games, same discards) the coach
  won 57.2%. The user: unify, and **"there should only be one AI ever for
  the game"** (saved to memory).
