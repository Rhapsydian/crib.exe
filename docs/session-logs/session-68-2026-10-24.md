## Problem-solving notes

- **Products of probabilities:** per-gatekeeper targets and a victory
  target interact (0.725^4 ≈ 28% before Heat); a ramp reconciles them.
- **A class outlier skews calibration:** Wraith's weakness made layers 1-2
  read harder than they were for the other five; fix the outlier, then
  retune.
- **Long fights from a loadout, not a Mod:** Firewall Prime's 11 hands came
  from two Suppress pieces walling Breach, not only No Way In.
- **Content that "does nothing" gets sold:** a passive Burner looked like
  Cold Wallet to the Shop's sell logic.
- **Renames surface leftovers:** changing Skeleton Key's id let the type
  checker find its old map code.
