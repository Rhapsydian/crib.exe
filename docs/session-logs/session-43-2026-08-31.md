## Decisions resolved live

1. **Session opener: sweep the new player-pool content first, or move
   straight to the enemy pool?** Recommended and confirmed: go straight to
   the enemy pool — a sweep now would measure a snapshot that won't
   survive four more roadmap phases before the real final analysis pass.
2. **Scope: audit the enemy-only catalog itself** (not the shared pool
   enemies also draw from). Confirmed real structural findings before
   proposing anything: Heat-gated content is unconditionally dead for
   enemies (no Heat source), `gaugeFillAbove` is a trap (cyclical, not a
   real rarity signal), several enemies needed ad hoc Neutral-pool patches
   because their kits had no payload crediting their own gauge, and
   chained (`afterSubroutineId`) triggers are actually *fine* for
   hand-authored enemy kits (unlike the player pool's problem) since
   co-presence is guaranteed at authoring time.
3. **New invariant proposed and confirmed, with a user amendment**: every
   enemy loadout needs a credit-capable payload, **unless explicitly
   designed to stall to the hand-20 attrition resolution**. Applied
   forward, not retroactively re-litigating the 8 existing session-28
   retrofits.
4. **Catalog scope, option (a) vs (b)**: reserve `enemy-subroutines.ts`
   strictly for content that's enemy-*structural* (uses a mechanic/signal
   no player piece could), not generic enemy-exclusive flavor. Confirmed
   — avoids quietly re-opening the player-pool-size scope just closed.
5. **New gatekeeper design rule, the user's own proposal**: every
   gatekeeper needs at least one bespoke, highly thematic piece. Checked
   coverage first (only 3 of 12 already had one, each a side effect of an
   earlier dead-content fix) rather than assuming. Confirmed
   **gatekeeper-only**, not elite/regular — a real structural reason, not
   just the user's stated preference: gatekeepers are a fixed,
   single-per-layer assignment, while regular/elite are a floor an
   identity can recur across several layers from (`magnitudeScalerFor`
   already treats them differently for exactly this reason).
6. **Authoring approach**: design and implement in the same sitting,
   given the scale (9 gatekeepers needing a piece, not a
   Mods/Burners-Events-sized multi-pool pass).

## Work completed

**11 new pieces authored in `enemy-subroutines.ts`** — one each for the 9
gatekeepers lacking a bespoke piece (The Concierge, Ghost Process,
Incident Response, Total Compromise, Adaptive Threat, Silent Corruption,
Null Session), two each for Quarantine Ward and Zero-Sum (both found to
share Kernel Panic's exact pre-fix dead-trigger problem — confirmed

into its gatekeeper's own existing passive or fixes a concrete finding

quota. Wired into `ENEMY_ROSTER` (`enemies.ts`).

**A real regression found and fixed, in three rounds, via direct
measurement**: the first-draft pieces collapsed `run.test.ts`'s full-run
integration test (must find ≥1 victory in 50 seeds) to 0/50.

- **Orphaned Thread (Ghost Process)**: originally a permanent,
  uncapped-in-practice raise to the *InitiativeGauge* threshold (default
  8, not the 50-point win gauge). `maxFiresPerCombat` turned out to be
  unenforced for this trigger kind at all — a real engine gap, not just a
  missing field on the piece. Fixed the engine gap (`triggers.ts`'s
  `isReady` now checks it universally, the one real choke point every
  normal firing path shares) *and* still had to redesign the piece itself
  twice — even capped, the threshold-raise stayed a real outlier
  (9/100 vs a Layer-1 sibling's 93/100); landed on a single-application
  temporary debuff instead of a permanent structural change.
- **Escalation Path (Incident Response)**: built on a false premise —
  claimed Highest Bidder's passive had nothing to buff, but Incident
  Response's existing kit already has two real `chainFinisherScaling`
  pieces. A third would have compounded a live mechanic. Redesigned onto
  a plain, non-escalating burst. Isolating this piece also surfaced a
  genuine *pre-existing*, out-of-scope finding: Incident Response's own
  baseline (nothing from this session at all) is only 6/100 — flagged for
  a future balance session, not fixed here.
- **Contagion Protocol/Cryo Lock (Quarantine Ward)**: unblocked the
  ward's own pre-existing Total Quarantine passive for the first time
  ever (it never fired while Epidemic/Cold Storage were dead) —
  `globalPulse` cadence decoupled the passive's initiative-nudge from real
  turn economy, amplifying it past anything it was tested against.
  Switched both to `castersTurnPulse` and gave Cryo Lock a real, attainable
  gating condition instead of also going unconditional from turn 1.
