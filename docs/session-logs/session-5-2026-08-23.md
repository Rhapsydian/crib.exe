# Session 5 — 2026-08-23

`/decision-session`, making session 3's illustrative Occurrence-trigger
example list concrete and complete.

Most of this session wasn't a creative fork — it was cataloging
Cribbage's actual scoring categories, fixed by rules already adopted in
Combat System: **8 base categories** (Fifteen; Pair, covering pair/pair
royal/double pair royal as magnitude; Run, covering run/double/triple/
quadruple as magnitude; Flush; His Nobs; His Heels — the rare,
dealer-only, starter-is-a-Jack case; Thirty-One; Go), each unified across
the play/pegging phase and the show/count phase rather than split into
separate trigger types, and scoped to the caster's own scoring events
(not the enemy's — that's Enemy-state's job).

After initial confirmation, the user raised a further refinement:
occurrence triggers shouldn't be limited to "fires on the very first
instance." Landed on **3 firing variations** any of the 8 categories can
use: **Instant** (today's default — fires on every instance
independently), **Threshold** (occurrences bank silently until N have
happened, then fires with a flat payload — the Occurrence-family
equivalent of an Accumulator's threshold), and **Scaling** (occurrences
bank up to a cap and the payload scales with how many actually banked —
the swingy, variance-rewarding option: a lucky streak of pairs right
before your turn hits much harder than one would).
