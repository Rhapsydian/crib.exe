## Decisions (user, live)

- **Burners: toggles, not a prompt.** A toggle under your gauges for
  each kind of combat Burner you carry; **only one armed at a time**
  (arming another disarms the first, the user's call); an armed Burner
  is used at the start of your next turn -- after "your turn" shows,
  before your subroutines fire -- then disarms.
- **Caches:** uncommon or rare only (2/3 uncommon, 1/3 rare).
- **Points-Hook wording:** "Every 20 points you score, its next fire
  Credits 1 more" (Steady Drip, Watchdog Timer, Memory Leak, Load
  Balancer) -- the old "since it last fired" misread as a reset.
- **Learn to play** remembers its own speed, starting slow.
- **Selling at Shops:** benched subroutines and carried Burners for half
  their buying price by rarity; **Cold Wallet**, a sell-only Burner (50
  Data), dropped but never sold.
- **Merge:** ranks and banked copies are shown; banked copies sell; a
  ranked subroutine sells at +25% a rank; and **a Safehouse Merge spends
  every banked copy at once** across all subroutines ("let's see how it
  feels and balances").
- **Re-balance:** re-tune the gatekeepers (not shrink Merge); then a
  Daemon Merge fix (ranked growing subroutines grow further); then buff
  Operator's and Warden's class Mods.

## Problem-solving notes

- **The Burner prompt's timing:** the engine already asked after the
  turn beat; the screen showed the prompt the moment the engine paused,
  while the steps leading to the turn were still animating. Answering
  automatically lets the replay show turn, Burner, fires in order.
- **The leftover card:** the hand comes from the engine's latest pause; a
  play that ends the fight brings no new pause, so the old hand stayed.
- **A wrong diagnosis caught by measuring:** Warden and Operator fell
  after the retune; the Daemon Merge fix (the first guess) changed
  nothing. Per-matchup pass rates showed the real cause -- the deep
  gatekeepers' big strength bumps (Null Session: Warden 68% -> 43%).
- **Ward is Warden's lever:** Suppress barely moved it; Ward did (Tarpit
  converts it to Credit).
- **Inline `npx tsx -e` hangs or prints nothing** in this setup -- use a
  script file.
